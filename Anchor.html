Anchor tag

The anchor tag, represented by the <> element in HTML, is used to create hyperlinks that allow users to navigate from one webpage to another or to specific sections within the same page. The anchor tag typically includes an href attribute that specifies the URL of the destination page or the target section. When a user clicks on the anchor text, they are directed to the specified location. Anchor tags can also be used to link to external websites, email addresses, or even trigger JavaScript functions. They are an essential part of web navigation and play a crucial role in connecting different parts of a website or linking to other resources on the internet.
